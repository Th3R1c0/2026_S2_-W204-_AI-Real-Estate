Due: Tue, 20 Oct 2026 23:59

Task Overview
In sprint 2, you will need to perform and create the artefacts of Sprint 0, Sprint 1, and provide evidence of code review. At the start of each sprint, you are expected to perform sprint planning like that of Sprint 0 (i.e., planning poker, new burndown chart, sprint goals, etc.). Refer to Assessment 1, if you are unsure what you need to do. Once you have completed the sprint planning, you will carry out the work required in the sprint and create the sprint artefacts (I.e., daily stand-up meeting minutes, unit testing, test-driven development, coding, etc. - refer back to Assessment 1 and 2, if you are unsure). In addition, the final deliverable will require evidence of code review.

Purpose
This submission relates to the following course outcome(s):
• Understand the role of software engineering processes and practices in managing the complexity, quality and timeliness of software development projects.
• Collaborate within a team to scope, plan, and manage a software development project.
• Control the quality, production, and appropriateness of project deliverables
• Display an increase in the range of development tools and technologies at their disposal.
